# Q2 NDA Playbook

## Core positions

| Topic | Preferred / acceptable | Redline or escalate |
|---|---|---|
| Mutuality | Mutual duties where disclosure is mutual | One-sided obligations in a mutual exchange |
| Parties | Full legal names and addresses | Blank or unclear identity |
| Effective date | Last-signature date or clear non-retroactive date | Retroactive or missing date |
| Confidential Information | Broad, includes oral/electronic/visual information and reasonable-person standard | Marked-only or trade-secret-only definition |
| Purpose | Evaluating, discussing, or pursuing the stated business relationship | Broad business, development, benchmarking, analytics, or competitive use |
| Use restriction | Solely for the stated Purpose | No restriction or broader rights |
| Care | Same care used for comparable information, not less than reasonable care | No objective standard |
| Representatives | Need-to-know; confidentiality duties; recipient responsibility | Free disclosure to affiliates, partners, contractors, or advisors |
| Compelled disclosure | Prompt prior notice to extent legally permitted; reasonable cooperation | No notice; consent requirement before complying with law |
| Unauthorized disclosure | Prompt notice of known unauthorized use/disclosure; reasonable mitigation support | No notice duty |
| Exclusions | Public domain; rightful possession; lawful third-party receipt; independent development without reference/use | Material deletion or unusable narrowing |
| Ownership / license | Discloser retains ownership; no express or implied license | IP assignment, ownership creep, implied license |
| Warranty | “As is” is sufficient; fuller disclaimer acceptable | Accuracy, completeness, fitness, or other warranties |
| Agreement term | Usually 2 years; clear end point | Indefinite term without reason |
| Confidentiality survival | 2–5 years; Q2 standard 5 years; trade secrets while legally protected | Indefinite for all information; no survival |
| Return / destruction | Written request; 5–10 business days; certification if requested | Immediate, no timeframe, or impossible purge |
| Backups | Routine archives/backups may remain subject to confidentiality | Absolute purge or retention without continuing duties |
| Equitable relief | Mutual; “may seek appropriate equitable relief” | Entitlement, one-sided relief, no-bond, embedded fees |
| Indemnity | None | Any NDA-breach indemnity |
| Fees | None preferred; mutual prevailing-party language can be tolerated | One-way or expanded enforcement costs |
| Governing law | New York preferred neutral compromise; Delaware or Texas acceptable; UK contextually acceptable | Other jurisdiction without business reason; unusual forum/arbitration |
| Affiliates | Define if used | Undefined affiliate(s) |
| Assignment | No assignment without consent is acceptable | Unexpected transfer rights or conflict with binding-effect language |
| No obligation to proceed | Express statement preferred but omission is not automatically material | Language committing a party to proceed |
| Residuals | None | Broad unaided-memory or residual-use rights |
| Restrictive covenants | None | Non-solicit, non-circumvent, no-contact, non-compete, exclusivity, standstill |
| Supersession | Narrowly limited to confidentiality for stated Purpose | Broad override of MSA, DPA, SOW, or existing NDA |
| E-signatures | Counterparts and electronic signatures accepted | Wet-ink-only without reason |
| Tri-party mechanics | Clear reciprocal discloser/recipient logic | Ambiguous disclosure paths or asymmetric duties |

## Decision rules

- **Good to sign:** No material issues; only immaterial cleanup.
- **Sign with redlines:** Usable NDA with targeted changes.
- **Not ready to sign:** Structural, confidentiality, remedies, duration, identity, or supersession issues should be fixed first.

## Standard review checks

Always check:
- NDA type and mutuality
- party names, addresses, and signature blocks
- effective date
- Confidential Information definition and exclusions
- purpose and use restriction
- care standard
- representatives and affiliates
- compelled disclosure
- unauthorized disclosure notice
- ownership and no license
- warranty disclaimer
- term and survival
- return/destruction and backups
- equitable relief
- indemnity and fee shifting
- restrictive covenants and residuals
- governing law
- assignment, amendment, waiver, severability, counterparts
- existing agreement / supersession risk
