# Q2 Standard and Fallback Language

Use only to draft a fix. Never use this file as evidence that language exists in the NDA.

## Reasonable-person standard
“or information that by its nature a reasonable person should understand to be confidential or proprietary”

## Independent development
“independently developed without reference to or use of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information”

## Third-party receipt
“rightfully received from a third party without breach of any duty of confidentiality owed to the Disclosing Party”

## Standard of care
“the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar importance, but in no event less than reasonable care”

## Representatives
“to its representatives who have a need to know such information for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those set forth herein; provided that Recipient remains responsible for their compliance”

## Compelled disclosure
“to the extent legally permitted, provide prompt prior written notice and reasonably cooperate, at the Disclosing Party's expense, in seeking a protective order or other appropriate remedy”

## Unauthorized disclosure
“promptly notify the Disclosing Party upon becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure and reasonably cooperate in mitigating or remedying it”

## No license
“No license or other rights, by implication or otherwise, are granted under any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property right.”

## Warranty fallback
“All Confidential Information is provided ‘AS IS.’”

## Term and survival
“This Agreement will remain in effect for two (2) years from the Effective Date. Each party's confidentiality obligations will continue for five (5) years after expiration or termination; provided that obligations for trade secrets will continue for so long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.”

## Return / destruction
“Within ten (10) business days after written request, Recipient will return or destroy the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information and, if requested, certify compliance in writing.”

## Backup carveout
“Recipient may retain automatically created archival or backup copies maintained in the ordinary course, subject to the continuing confidentiality obligations of this Agreement and destruction in accordance with Recipient's normal retention cycle.”

## Equitable relief
“Either party may seek appropriate equitable relief for any actual or threatened breach of this Agreement.”

## No obligation to proceed
“Nothing in this Agreement obligates either party to proceed with any proposed transaction or relationship.”

## Narrow supersession protection
“This Agreement supersedes prior confidentiality understandings only with respect to disclosures made for the Purpose and does not amend or supersede any other agreement between the parties.”
