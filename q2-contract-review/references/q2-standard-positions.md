# Q2 Standard Contractual Positions
## Extracted from Q2 Templates (2023–2025)
 
This file documents Q2's actual template positions extracted from:
- Master Partner Agreement v. 2024
- Reseller Program Addendum v. 2024
- Channel Partner Program Addendum v. 2024
- Partner Accelerator Program Agreement v. 2024
- Integration Agreement Template (2022)
- Referral and Integration Agreement
---
 
## 1. Governing Law & Venue
 
**Q2 Standard (all agreements):**
- Governing law: **State of Texas** (substantive laws, without conflict of laws principles)
- Venue: **Federal or state courts in Travis County, Texas** (or "State of Texas")
- No arbitration clause in standard templates
- UN Convention on Contracts for International Sale of Goods is **expressly excluded**
- UCITA (Uniform Computer Information Transactions Act) is **expressly excluded**
- Partner Accelerator Agreement adds: **jury trial waiver** and Q2 entitled to attorneys' fees if Q2 prevails
**Flag if counterparty proposes:**
- 🔴 Non-Texas governing law (Delaware, New York, California, etc.)
- 🔴 Mandatory arbitration
- 🔴 Venue outside Travis County or State of Texas
**Note:** The Integration Agreement template (2022) uses **New York law** — this is an older template that predates the Q2 standard. If reviewing an integration agreement, Texas law is still preferred but New York may be acceptable if legacy template.

## 2. Liability Cap
 
**Q2 Standard (Master Partner Agreement § 7.2):**
> Mutual cap at **amounts paid or payable by Partner to Q2 for the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim** ("General Liability Cap")
 
- Cap is **mutual** — applies to both parties equally
- Referral and Integration Agreement uses the same formula but only caps Company's liability; Q2's liability is uncapped on confidentiality breach
**Cap Carve-Outs (not subject to cap) — Q2 Standard (Master Partner Agreement § 7.2):**
 
These are the **authoritative Q2 carve-outs**. The Reseller Addendum § 8 contains broader unlimited
exposure language (also carving out security, ADA/WCAG, and indemnification from the consequential
damages waiver) — this is **not Q2's preferred position** and should not be offered or accepted
in any contract review. Always revert to MPA carve-outs.
 
Approved carve-outs from the 12-month cap:
- Breach of **confidentiality obligations**
- Breach of **IP/proprietary rights**
- **Indemnification obligations**
- **Gross negligence or willful misconduct**
- **Payment obligations** (never capped)
**Do NOT offer or accept** unlimited/uncapped exposure for:
- Security addendum obligations (Reseller Addendum language — not Q2's preferred position)
- ADA/WCAG compliance (Reseller Addendum language — not Q2's preferred position)
- Removal of the consequential damages waiver for security breaches
**Flag if counterparty proposes:**
- 🔴 Uncapped liability for Q2 but capped for counterparty (asymmetric cap)
- 🔴 Cap below 12 months of fees
- 🔴 Missing mutual carve-outs for the MPA-approved categories above
- 🔴 Carve-outs broader than the MPA list (e.g., all data-related claims, all regulatory violations)
- 🔴 Any clause exposing Q2 to unlimited liability beyond the five MPA carve-outs

## 3. Consequential Damages Waiver
 
**Q2 Standard (Master Partner Agreement § 7.1):**
> Mutual waiver of **indirect, special, incidental, or consequential damages**, including lost profits, revenue, business, savings, data, or use
 
**Same carve-outs apply** as the liability cap (confidentiality breach, IP breach, indemnification obligations, gross negligence/willful misconduct, payment obligations)
 
**Flag if counterparty proposes:**
- 🔴 One-sided waiver (counterparty waives nothing, Q2 waives everything)
- 🔴 Missing carve-outs for confidentiality, IP, or gross negligence
- 🟡 Asymmetric carve-outs (counterparty has broader carve-outs than Q2)

## 4. Confidentiality
 
**Q2 Standard Definition (Master Partner Agreement § 1.4):**
Confidential Information includes information that is:
1. Marked "Confidential" or "Proprietary" in writing
2. Identified as confidential at time of oral disclosure
3. By its nature reasonably considered confidential
**Also included by definition:** terms of the Agreement, SDK/APIs, Documentation, Private Information, Fees/pricing, product roadmaps.
 
**Obligations (§ 4.1):**
- Mutual — each Receiving Party protects Disclosing Party's CI
- Use only to fulfill purpose of Agreement
- Disclose only to Representatives with need to know, bound by written confidentiality obligations
- Prompt notice of actual or suspected unauthorized disclosure
**Return/Destruction (§ 4.5 / § 5.3):**
- Within **10 days** of expiration or termination
- Destroy and permanently delete all copies
- Exception: one archival copy for compliance/enforcement/defense, subject to ongoing confidentiality obligations
- Written certification from officer on request
**Survival:**
- Q2 templates: confidentiality obligations **survive termination indefinitely** (or "for so long as retained" for archival copies)
- Reseller Security Addendum: Personal Data protection obligations "cannot be terminated"
**Flag if counterparty proposes:**
- 🔴 No return/destroy obligation
- 🔴 Confidentiality survival less than 3 years post-termination
- 🔴 Missing written certification obligation
- 🔴 Counterparty retaining broad rights to use Q2 CI post-termination
- 🟡 Return/destroy window longer than 30 days (Q2 standard is 10 days)
- 🟡 Missing "need to know" restriction on disclosure to Representatives
**Standard Exclusions (acceptable — these appear in all Q2 templates):**
1. Information in public domain at time of disclosure (not through Receiving Party's fault)
2. Known to Receiving Party without restriction at time of disclosure
3. Disclosed with Disclosing Party's prior written approval
4. Independently developed without reference to CI
5. Becomes known from third party without breach
6. Required by Applicable Law (with notice to Disclosing Party and cooperation on protective order)

## 5. IP Ownership
 
**Q2 Standard (Master Partner Agreement § 4.3 and § 4.4):**
 
- **Q2 Materials** (Q2 platform, trademarks, IP, documentation, derivatives): **Q2 exclusively owns**; Partner may not file any IP applications related to Q2 Materials
- **Partner Materials** (Partner's IP, documentation, trademarks, Partner Offerings): **Partner exclusively owns**; Q2 may not file any IP applications related to Partner Materials
- **Reservation of Rights:** No implied licenses; each party reserves all rights not expressly granted
**Partner Accelerator (§ 6.1):**
- SDK/APIs: **Q2 owns** exclusively
- Applications developed by Partner using SDK/APIs: **Partner owns**; Q2 obtains no rights to them
- Q2's right to continue development of similar technology is preserved (non-blocking)
**License grants in templates are always:**
- Non-exclusive
- Non-transferable (except as permitted under assignment provision)
- Revocable (trademark licenses)
- Limited to specific purpose and territory (United States)
- Term-limited (expire at end of agreement)
**Flag if counterparty proposes:**
- 🔴 Counterparty claiming ownership of any Q2 platform, technology, or data processed through Q2 services
- 🔴 Counterparty claiming ownership of deliverables that incorporate Q2's IP
- 🔴 Broad license grants to counterparty that survive termination without limitation
- 🔴 Right to sublicense without Q2 consent
- 🔴 Counterparty using Q2 data to develop competing products or train AI models
- 🟡 License grants without "non-exclusive" and "non-transferable" restrictions
- 🟡 Missing reservation of rights clause

## 6. Indemnification
 
**Q2 Standard (Reseller Addendum § 6.1; Channel Partner Addendum § 6.1):**
 
Mutual indemnification for third-party Actions arising from:
- (a) **IP infringement** by the indemnifying party's products/services
- (b) **Breach of confidentiality** (§ 4 of Master Partner Agreement)
- (c) **Compliance with laws** (§ 9.11 of Master Partner Agreement)
- (d) **Gross negligence or willful misconduct**
Partner Accelerator adds:
- Partner indemnifies Q2 for the Program (broader — not limited to IP infringement)
- Q2 indemnifies Partner only for IP infringement by SDK/APIs/Q2 Services
**Indemnification Procedure (Master Partner Agreement § 6.1):**
- Indemnitee: (a) prompt written notice (late notice only reduces obligation if it impairs defense); (b) cooperate at Indemnifying Party's expense; (c) permit Indemnifying Party to control defense
- Indemnifying Party **cannot settle without Indemnitee's prior written consent** if settlement requires Indemnitee to admit liability
- Indemnitee may participate at own expense
**Exclusions from IP Indemnification (§ 6.2):**
No obligation to indemnify if Action arises from:
1. Modification not made by indemnifying party
2. Combination with products not provided by indemnifying party
3. Continued use after notified of infringement
4. Content provided by Indemnitee or its clients
5. Indemnifying party's compliance with Indemnitee's specifications
**Flag if counterparty proposes:**
- 🔴 One-sided indemnification (only Q2 indemnifies)
- 🔴 Missing mutual IP indemnification
- 🔴 Counterparty can settle without Q2 consent even if Q2 must admit liability
- 🔴 Uncapped indemnification obligation for Q2 with no equivalent for counterparty
- 🟡 Missing the standard IP indemnification exclusions (modification, combination, continued use)
- 🟡 Indemnification trigger broader than "third-party Actions" (e.g., covers first-party losses)

## 7. Payment Terms
 
**Q2 Standard:**
- Partner pays Q2 invoices within **30 days** of invoice receipt (Master Partner Agreement § 3.1)
- Late payment interest: **1.5% per month** (or maximum permitted by law, whichever is lower)
- Q2 right to suspend services if fees not paid within **10 days** after due date (without liability to Q2)
- Good faith dispute: Partner delivers written notice within **30 days** of invoice receipt, describing basis and amount withheld
- Taxes: Partner bears all taxes; Q2 honors valid exemption certificates
**Reseller/Channel arrangements:**
- Q2 invoices Partner on the **3rd business day after end of each calendar month**
- Partner pays Q2 invoices per Master Partner Agreement terms (Net 30)
**Flag if counterparty proposes:**
- 🟡 Net 60 or longer payment terms
- 🟡 No late payment interest
- 🟡 Dispute period shorter than 30 days
- 🟡 No suspension right for non-payment

## 8. Term & Termination
 
**Q2 Standard (Master Partner Agreement § 8.1):**
 
**Termination for Cause:**
- Written notice + **30-day cure period** for material breach
- Immediate termination for: dissolution/liquidation, insolvency, bankruptcy, general assignment for benefit of creditors, regulatory direction
**Regulatory Termination (Q2-specific right):**
- Q2 may terminate immediately if ordered by state/federal regulatory authority, or if such authority threatens administrative action against Q2
**Auto-Renewal:**
- Channel Partner Addendum: auto-renews for **12-month Renewal Terms**; non-renewal notice required **180 days** before expiration
- Reseller Addendum: same — 180 days notice
- Referral Agreement: non-renewal notice required **60 days** before expiration
- Integration Agreement: non-renewal notice required **90 days** before expiration
**Initial Term:**
- Channel Partner Addendum: **3-year** initial term
- Reseller/Accelerator: term length left blank (to be negotiated)
**Post-Termination Obligations:**
- Outstanding Clients: services continue for remainder of their contract terms (or 1 year for Channel Partner Addendum)
- All licenses terminate on termination except as needed for Outstanding Clients
- Confidentiality, IP, indemnification, limitation of liability survive
**Flag if counterparty proposes:**
- 🟡 Cure period shorter than 30 days
- 🟡 No regulatory termination right for Q2
- 🟡 Auto-renewal notice window shorter than 60 days (Q2 standard ranges from 60–180 days depending on contract type)
- 🟡 No Outstanding Client runoff provision
- 🟡 Termination for convenience right that allows counterparty to terminate Q2 Services without notice

## 9. Assignment
 
**Q2 Standard (Master Partner Agreement § 9.4):**
- Neither party may assign without prior written consent of the other
- **Exception:** Assignment permitted without consent in connection with merger, consolidation, corporate reorganization, sale of all/substantially all assets, stock sale, or name change — provided:
  - Assigning party gives reasonable notice
  - Other party has right to terminate immediately if assignee is a **Competitor**
- Any other attempted assignment is **null and void**
**Partner Accelerator (§ 12.3) — Q2-favorable variation:**
- Q2 may assign without Partner consent in M&A scenarios
- Partner needs Q2 consent in all cases
**Flag if counterparty proposes:**
- 🟡 Unrestricted assignment right for counterparty with no consent or notice
- 🟡 No competitor termination right for Q2 if counterparty assigns to a Q2 competitor
- 🟡 Assignment provisions that are more favorable to counterparty than to Q2

## 10. Insurance
 
**Q2 Standard (Master Partner Agreement § 9.1):**
 
Both parties must maintain:
1. **Commercial General Liability:** $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate
2. **Workers' Compensation:** statutory + $1M Employer's Liability
3. **Umbrella Liability:** $5M per occurrence and aggregate
4. **Professional Liability / Tech E&O with Cyber:** $10M per claim (Master Partner Agreement); $5M (Accelerator)
Requirements:
- A.M. Best rating "A-VII" or better
- Each party named as **additional insured** under the other's policies
- 30 days prior notice of cancellation or material reduction
- "Claims made" policies: maintain coverage for **3 years post-termination**
**Flag if counterparty proposes:**
- 🟡 Coverage limits below Q2 minimums
- 🟡 Missing additional insured endorsement
- 🟡 Insurance carrier rated below A-VII

## 11. Data / Security
 
**Q2 Standard (Reseller Security Addendum / Channel Partner Security Addendum):**
 
Key obligations on the data-receiving party:
- Maintain comprehensive written information security program
- SOC 1 Type II and SOC 2 Type II reports annually
- Annual penetration testing (external) and vulnerability scanning (weekly minimum)
- Encryption in transit and at rest (AES or equivalent)
- MFA on all internet-accessible applications
- IP filtering against OFAC-restricted countries
- Security Breach notification: **within 24 hours** of discovery/suspicion (Reseller); within **48 hours** (Integration Agreement)
- Annual Q2 audit right (60 days notice, once per year)
**Personal Data:**
- Governed by GLBA and Consumer Data Protection Laws
- Stored only in physically and logically secure environments
- Not copied to portable media without encryption
- Partner cannot communicate with Clients/End Users about a breach without Q2's prior written consent
**Flag if counterparty proposes:**
- 🔴 Breach notification window longer than 72 hours
- 🔴 No SOC 2 Type II requirement
- 🔴 No Q2 audit right
- 🟡 Breach notification excludes Q2 data or Q2 client data
- 🟡 Encryption carve-outs broader than necessary for debugging

## 12. Dispute Resolution
 
**Q2 Standard:**
- Good faith negotiation between senior executives for **30 days** before formal proceedings
- Either party may initiate formal proceedings if unresolved after 30 days
- No mandatory arbitration — court proceedings are default
- Discussions during dispute resolution are **Confidential Information**
**Flag if counterparty proposes:**
- 🔴 Mandatory arbitration (waiver of court rights)
- 🔴 Class action waiver that is unfavorable to Q2
- 🟡 Dispute resolution as a condition precedent to litigation (blocking Q2 from urgent relief)
---
 
## 13. Force Majeure
 
**Q2 Standard (Master Partner Agreement § 9.15):**
- Excuses: civil disorders, wars, terrorism, strikes, labor disputes, fires, floods, acts of God, or other causes not within party's reasonable control and not caused by party's fault/negligence
- **Payment obligations are NOT excused** by force majeure
- Business continuity/disaster recovery failures are NOT excused if BCP/DR should have reasonably mitigated the event
- If Force Majeure continues for **45+ consecutive calendar days**, other party may terminate the affected Addendum by written notice
**Flag if counterparty proposes:**
- 🟡 Force majeure excusing payment obligations
- 🟡 No 45-day outside date after which Q2 can terminate
