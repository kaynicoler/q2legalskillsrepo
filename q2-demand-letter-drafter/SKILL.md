# Q2 Demand Letter Drafter
 
Use this skill to draft Q2 Software, Inc. notices of breach, demands for payment, final demands, and post-cure payment-default notices arising from customer or partner agreements.
 
The goal is to produce formal, legally precise, contract-anchored drafts that mirror Q2 precedent and are suitable for internal legal review.
 
## Required Review
 
Review all uploaded agreements, order forms, amendments, invoices, statements, and correspondence before drafting.
 
Extract:
 
- Agreement name, effective date, parties, and document hierarchy.
- Payment obligations.
- Default, breach, cure, suspension, termination, and remedies provisions.
- Finance-charge or late-fee provisions.
- Invoice numbers, issue dates, due dates, service periods, and balances.
- Prior reminders or follow-up communications.
Determine document hierarchy in this order when applicable: Master Agreement, Order Form, Amendment, incorporated terms, invoices, correspondence.
 
Use bracketed review notes for missing or uncertain facts, including examples such as `[Review: missing due date]`, `[Review: confirm governing agreement name]`, and `[Review: cure period not found]`.
 
## Drafting Rules
 
Draft in full paragraphs only. Do not use bullets inside the letter.
 
Use the actual contractual cure period when stated. If no cure period is found, default to ten (10) business days. Preserve “working days” if the agreement uses that term.
 
Use precise section references only. Do not cite a section unless verified.
 
Keep the tone firm, factual, professional, and legally conservative. Do not speculate or add unsupported legal conclusions.
 
## Preferred Q2 Style
 
Use Q2’s preferred phrasing:
 
“Please treat this as Q2’s final demand for payment.”
 
“This letter constitutes formal notice of breach.”
 
“Q2 hereby demands immediate payment…”
 
“Failure to remit payment within the stated timeframe will leave Q2 with no alternative but to pursue all remedies available under the Agreement and applicable law.”
 
Avoid redundant conclusory statements. Avoid “second default.” Avoid “is hereby afforded.”
 
Prefer “once again” and “has seven (7) working days” where applicable.
 
Omit a full reservation-of-rights paragraph unless specifically requested.
 
## Letter Structure
 
Use this structure:
 
Date.
 
Customer or partner name and address.
 
Delivery method.
 
Subject line.
 
Salutation.
 
Agreement-reference paragraph.
 
Statement of breach or non-payment paragraph.
 
Finance-charge and Contract Amount paragraph, if applicable.
 
Demand and cure paragraph.
 
Final-demand language.
 
Contact line to q2legal@q2.com.
 
Signature block.
 
Default signatory:
 
Heidi Rager
Vice President, Deputy General Counsel
Q2 Software, Inc.
 
Use Scott Kerr, Associate General Counsel, only when instructed or supported by context.
 
## Output Format
 
First provide concise review notes identifying any missing or uncertain facts.
 
Then provide the full letter content.
